▶Get Transcript

The words from any video, in one tap

Watching a reel, a podcast or a voice note on your iPhone? Tap Share → Get Transcript. You get every word, and it can go straight to ChatGPT or Claude to summarise it, pull out the steps, or write a post.

Get the free app

Get it on the App Store

Install it and open it once. That's all the setup. No code, nothing to paste.

2

Use it

On any video, tap Share, then tap Get Transcript in the row of apps.

Don't see it? Swipe the row of apps to the end, tap More, and turn on Get Transcript. In Facebook or Instagram, tap Share, then More first.

3

Pick where it goes

A few seconds later you choose what it should do: summarise, key points and action steps, a social post, translate to Bangla, or your own words. Then send it to ChatGPT or Claude, or just copy it.

Your own prompts live in the app's My prompts tab.

No sound to share? Use a screen recording

If something is playing inside an app with no Share button, turn on Screen Recording in Control Centre while it plays. Then open the recording in Photos, tap Share → Get Transcript.

Free: up to 20 transcripts a day. Transcripts are deleted from our server after a day. On Android? Get the Android version.
