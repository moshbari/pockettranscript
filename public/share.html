On any video, tap Share, scroll down, and tap Get Transcript.

Don't see it? At the bottom of the Share menu tap Edit Actions and turn it on. In Facebook or Instagram, tap Share, then More first.

4

Pick where it goes

A few seconds later you choose Ask ChatGPT, Ask Claude or Just copy it, then what it should do: summarise, key points and action steps, a social post, translate to Bangla, or your own words.

For ChatGPT or Claude, have that app on your iPhone and be logged in. The words are always copied too, so you can paste them anywhere.

No sound to share? Use a screen recording

If something is playing inside an app with no Share button, turn on Screen Recording in Control Centre while it plays. Then open the recording in Photos, tap Share → Get Transcript.

Want YouTube too?

YouTube only hands its captions to a real computer, so YouTube needs a free helper in Chrome on your Mac or PC. Set up the computer helper, then connect this phone at pocket.99dfy.com (in this same browser). Your Share button starts doing YouTube too. Nothing to reinstall.
