▶Get Transcript

The words from any video, in one tap

Watching a reel, a podcast or a voice note on your iPhone? Tap Share → Get Transcript. You get every word, and it can go straight to ChatGPT or Claude to summarise it, pull out the steps, or write a post.

1

Add it to your iPhone

Open this page on your iPhone and tap the button.

＋ Add to my iPhone

The Shortcuts app opens. Tap Add Shortcut.
If it asks for a code, just leave it empty and tap Add Shortcut again. That's it.

2

Use it

On any video, tap Share, scroll down, and tap Get Transcript.

Don't see it? At the bottom of the Share menu tap Edit Actions and turn it on. In Facebook or Instagram, tap Share, then More first.

3

Pick where it goes

A few seconds later you choose Ask ChatGPT, Ask Claude or Just copy it, then what it should do: summarise, key points and action steps, a social post, translate to Bangla, or your own words.

For ChatGPT or Claude, have that app on your iPhone and be logged in. The words are always copied too, so you can paste them anywhere.

No sound to share? Use a screen recording

If something is playing inside an app with no Share button, turn on Screen Recording in Control Centre while it plays. Then open the recording in Photos, tap Share → Get Transcript.

Want YouTube too?

YouTube only hands its captions to a real computer, so YouTube needs a free helper in Chrome on your Mac or PC. Set up the computer helper, connect your phone at pocket.99dfy.com, then tap Install the Share button there. It fills in your code for you.
