1Get the words

Every good post starts with something you watched.

You're scrolling YouTube on the bus, or in your lunch break, and a video stops you. A clear idea, a great story, a point your buyers need to hear. It would make a brilliant post, in your own words, for your own people.

But a video is sound, not words. To write about it you'd have to watch it again with a notepad, pause every ten seconds, and type. On a phone, nobody does that. The idea is gone by tomorrow.
So skip the typing. Tap Share on the video, choose Get Transcript, and a few seconds later every word is there, with the title and the link. It works for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X, and for your own voice notes and screen recordings too.

Try it now

  1. Open a video in YouTube and tap Share.
  2. Swipe the row of apps to the right, tap More, and choose Get Transcript.
  3. The first time, tap Edit and the green + next to Get Transcript, so it sits at the front of the row from now on.
  4. Wait a few seconds. You'll see how many words it found, with Read it all and Copy it.

2Hand them to ChatGPT

The words are only as good as what you ask for.

Under the words sits a list of ready-made instructions for faceless text posts, the kind you post without ever showing your face: a Facebook post in Bangla and English, a post with an audience check, a story with a character and a villain, a thread, a comment, a summary, the key points. Pick one, and the instruction plus the whole transcript go to ChatGPT together.

Copying, switching apps, pasting the transcript, then typing a good prompt from memory: that's where most people give up, or end up with a bland "here's a summary" post that sounds like everyone else's.
One tap does all of it. Tap the instruction you want: everything is copied and ChatGPT opens. Tap the message box, Paste, and send. Then the post prompts do something most people never ask for: ChatGPT checks its own post against your audience, finds what's wrong, and writes it again. From one video you get three versions, ending with a story that has a character and a villain.

Your first post

  1. Under Ask, keep ChatGPT picked and tap FB post + audience check + character & villain story (third from the top).
  2. In ChatGPT, tap the message box, Paste, then the send arrow.
  3. Let it write, check and rewrite. The last version is the story you want. AI can be wrong, so read it: you're the editor.
  4. Ask for an image with your image prompt. Not quite right? Ask for another version that speaks to your audience's pain and what they want, and keep it simple.
  5. Download the image and copy the story. In Facebook: add the image, paste, Next, Post.

Have instructions of your own that work for you? Save them in My prompts with a name. They show at the top of the list after every transcript, on your phone and on this website.

3A thread from any video

One video, a whole week of content.

A thread keeps people reading: one strong opening post, then a comment after comment that each adds one more idea. It's one of the best ways to stay in front of people on Facebook and X.

Writing one by hand means splitting the idea into pieces, keeping each piece short, and making them flow. It's an hour of work for one post.
Choose Thread post (X + Facebook) instead. ChatGPT writes an X version first, then a Facebook version: the main post plus every comment, each in its own box with a copy button. The last comment is your call to action.

4Posts with no video

A post of words has no transcript to get.

Some of the best things on Facebook, LinkedIn and Threads are written posts, and the gold is often in the comments under them. Share one of those to Get Transcript, and it knows there's nothing to transcribe.

The usual fix is screenshots: one, scroll, another, scroll, another. Then you pick them out of your gallery in the right order, attach them to ChatGPT one by one, and hope it can read them all.
Get Transcript records it instead. It offers Record this post. You scroll through the post and the comments you want, and stop. It joins the recording into one long picture, cuts it into screen-sized pages, and reads every word on them. The pages also go into your Photos.

Record a post

  1. On the post, tap Share → Get Transcript → Record this post.
  2. iPhone: tap Start Broadcast, then ◀ at the top left to go back to the post. Android: tap Start.
  3. Scroll down slowly through the post and the comments you want.
  4. iPhone: tap the red time at the top, then Stop. Android: pull down the notifications and tap Stop.
  5. Tap the "Post recorded" note. The post is read and ready.

No Share button handy? On the app's home screen, tap Capture a text post: record and scroll, start recording, and open the post yourself.

5A comment that brings people back

The cheapest leads are under other people's posts.

Big creators pull thousands of people into their comment sections every day. Those people are already interested, and already reading. Show up there with a comment that adds something real, and some of them click on you, read your profile and message you. You're borrowing traffic, and it costs you nothing.

Borrow it like a good guest, though. A "DM me", a link, a pitch, and the creator bans you. And a genuinely helpful comment takes thought: read the whole post, read what others said, find something worth adding. After a full day at work, doing that well three times a day is exactly what never happens.
After a post is read, Comment on this post sits at the top of the list. Tap it, Paste in ChatGPT, send. It has the whole post and the comments as text, so you don't attach a single screenshot. It writes a humble, supportive comment that adds value, checks its own quality, and puts the post's link on the first line, so you can always find the post again.

From post to comment

  1. Record the post (chapter 4), then tap "Post recorded".
  2. Tap Comment on this post.
  3. In ChatGPT: Paste, send. Read the comment it writes.
  4. Copy it, go back to the post (or tap the link), paste and post.

Want to see the whole post as text first? Tap Read it all. You can select and copy any part of it.

6Save it for later

The best ideas show up when you're busy.

You're scrolling in a queue, between meetings, half-watching. You see a video or a post you'd love to use. Right now you can't stop and write.

Screenshots and "I'll remember it" don't work. By tonight it's buried under a hundred other things, and Facebook won't show you that post again.
Tap Share → Save for later. One tap, a quick "Saved", and you're back to scrolling. When you have time, open the Saved tab: everything you kept is waiting. Tap one to get its words and write your post. Mark it done when you've used it.

Keep it in one tap

  1. Tap Share on a post or video.
  2. Choose Save for later in the list under the app icons (tap View More the first time).
  3. Later: open Get Transcript → Saved.

7Write it here, see the Community

Write without leaving the app, and never run out of ideas.

Under every transcript there's also Make it here. Pick a post, a thread or a comment, and Get Transcript writes it for you on the spot. No copying, no pasting, no ChatGPT. Every day you get one post, one thread and one comment free.

Writing on your own gets lonely, and some mornings the ideas just dry up. You open Facebook, stare at the box, and post nothing.
That's what the Community tab is for. It shows what other people made from the videos and posts they found, each one with the name its maker picked. Scroll it for ideas, see what's working for others, and use it to spark your own. Your own pieces are under Mine.

The Community fills up on its own, too. When you use one of the post, thread or comment prompts in ChatGPT and come back to Get Transcript, the answer you copied is added for you. A note says "Added to the Community", with Undo right there if you'd rather keep that one to yourself.

Write one here

  1. Get the words from a video or a post (chapters 1 and 4).
  2. Under the transcript, tap Make it here and choose Post, Thread or Comment.
  3. The first time, agree to the Community rules and pick the name people will see.
  4. Read it, copy it, post it. It's kept under Community → Mine.

Rather not share what you make in ChatGPT? Turn off Share what I make in ChatGPT in Settings. You can change your name there too.

1Ready-made RANT, your way New

Want it funny? Want it to roast? Just tap.

Ready-made RANT watches the video for you and writes short RANTs at the best moments. All you do is read them out.

The catch: every RANT came out sounding the same. Some days you want to joke. Some days you want to hit hard.

So now you pick the mood first, and you can tell it exactly what you want.

  • Pick a tone: Normal, Funny, Roast the creator, Roast the system, Wise comedian or Straight talk.
  • Type your own rule, like “2 lines max, only 2 RANTs”.
  • Write them in English or বাংলা.
  • The app remembers your choice for next time. All of it is optional.
2One tap from anywhere New

See a video. Tap once. Your RANT is being written.

Good videos show up everywhere: in YouTube, in Facebook, or in an old RANT you already made.

Before, you had to open the app, paste the link, wait, and then go looking for the Ready-made button.

These days the button sits right where you already are.

  • From the Share button in YouTube, Facebook and other apps.
  • On every card in My RANTs.
  • Right after you paste a link.
  • Make a copy of any RANT to try it a new way. The first one stays safe.
3Pick your part

Only want 10 seconds of a 10-minute video?

Most videos have one great bit. That's the bit you actually want to talk about.

Here's the problem: talking over the whole video makes your RANT long, and long videos get skipped.

That's why you can keep just your part and leave the rest out.

  • Drag the yellow handles. Hold one still to zoom in.
  • Or tap Mark it live: tap Start and Stop while it plays.
  • Want three bits? Tap + Add this part for each one.
  • The video stops at the end of each part, so it's your turn to talk.
  • The bits in between are cut out for you.
4Pick by words

Remember what they said, but not when?

You heard a line in the video and you want to talk about it.

Finding it by dragging back and forth takes forever.

Search for the words instead, and the app finds the moment.

  • Type a word. Every line with that word shows up.
  • Tap the first line, then the last line. That's your part.
  • Fine-tune it with the yellow handles if you want.
5Oops you can see New

Messed up a sentence? Tap Oops and keep going.

Everyone trips over words. You don't need to stop and start again.

Until now, you couldn't tell if Oops had worked. Was the mistake gone, or still in your video? You just had to hope.

Now every Oops shows up as a red block you can check yourself.

  • Tap Oops, take a breath, and say the sentence again.
  • In Cut a bit, each Oops is red, with an orange Oops tag.
  • Tap Play this cut to hear exactly what goes.
  • Wrong spot? Move the edges, or tap Put it back.
6Blur what shouldn't be seen

Hide a face, a logo, or anything not modest.

Some videos show things you don't want in yours.

Cutting those parts out breaks the story, and then your RANT makes no sense.

Blur them instead. The picture goes soft and the sound keeps playing.

  • Blur the video: tap Blur while you watch, or mark the parts later in Review.
  • Portrait blur: you stay sharp and the room behind you goes soft, like the iPhone's Portrait camera.
  • You see it live on the camera while you film. You choose how much.
7Finish the whole video here

End with what people should do next.

A good video ends by telling people what to do: message me, click the link, follow me.

That used to mean a second editing app, and usually a computer too.

Here, it's one card in Review.

  • CTA clip at the end: record it reading your script on the teleprompter, or pick it from Photos, Files or a link like Tella.
  • The app remembers it for your next RANT.
  • More clips: add clips before or after your RANT, in any order.
  • No other editing app needed.
8Fix one thing, keep the rest

Spotted one mistake? Fix just that.

You watch your finished RANT and one talk isn't right.

Starting again from zero throws away all your good work.

So you only change the part that's wrong.

  • Replace one talk. Everything else stays.
  • Film my part again: re-film it, and keep your CTA, clips, captions and look.
  • Keep watching: add more talks after where it ended.
  • Do it again or Make a copy: every version is kept.
9Your Facebook post, already written

The video is done. The post is too.

A video needs words next to it when you post it.

After making the video you're tired, and the post is the part most people skip.

The app writes it for you from what you said in your RANT.

  • A post comes with every RANT.
  • Copy it in one tap.
  • Don't love it? Tap New version.
  • Older posts are kept, right in My RANTs.
10Batch render New

Film ten RANTs. Tap one button. Walk away.

The fastest way to work is to film many RANTs in one go, then make them all at once.

Making them one at a time meant sitting by your phone, waiting for each one to finish.

This time, tick them all and let our server do the rest.

  • In My RANTs, tap Select and tick your RANTs.
  • Tap Batch render. They're made one by one.
  • Close the app. You get a notification as each one is ready.
  • Open the app and they're saved to your Photos.

Is it really free?

Yes. YouTube videos have no limit. Posts, voice notes and other videos can be read up to 20 times a day. Make it here writes one post, one thread and one comment for you every day, free.

Who sees what I put in the Community?

Everyone who uses the app, with the name you picked. Answers you copy from ChatGPT with a post, thread or comment prompt are added for you, and the "Added to the Community" note has an Undo button. You can delete your own pieces any time, or turn off "Share what I make in ChatGPT" in Settings.

Do I need my computer on to make a RANT?

No. The video is fetched and your RANT is put together on our server, in 1080p. Your phone is all you need, and you don't have to keep the app open while it's being made.

Is RANT on Android?

Yes. It's in Get Transcript for Android 1.8: download the newest app from the Android install card on this page. On iPhone it's in version 1.0.5, which is with Apple for review right now.

Does the Community see my RANT recordings?

No. The Community's RANTs list only shows the title and link of the video you're talking about, so others can RANT on it too. Your recordings stay yours, and you choose where to post the finished video.

What if the app closes in the middle of a RANT?

Nothing is lost. Each RANT is saved as you go and kept in My RANTs, so you open it again and carry on.

Do I need ChatGPT Plus?

No. The free ChatGPT app works. You can also pick Claude instead, or tap Copy it and paste the words anywhere you like.

Does my screen recording get uploaded?

No. The recording stays on your phone and is turned into pages there. Only those pages (pictures of the post) are sent to be read, and the pages are also saved in your Photos.

Which apps does it work with?

Videos from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X, plus voice notes and recordings from your phone. Record this post works for written posts on Facebook, LinkedIn and Threads, and for anything else you can scroll through.

Will ChatGPT get the post right?

Most of the time, and the prompts make it check and score its own work. But AI can be wrong, so always read what it wrote before you post it. You're the editor.

I used the old Share button shortcut or this website before. What changed?

The app replaces the shortcut, and everything you had still works. The website version is still here: tap Web app at the top of this page.
