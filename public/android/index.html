The words from any video, on Android

Watching a reel, a video or a voice note? Tap Share → Get Transcript. You get every word, and one more tap sends it to ChatGPT or Claude.

Step 1 · Add it to your phone (once)
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮ (top right), then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Tap Install.

That's what puts Get Transcript in your Share menu.

Step 2 · Use it
  1. On any video (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube), tap Share.
  2. Tap Get Transcript. Not there? Tap More first.
  3. Pick what to do, then pick ChatGPT or Claude.
Or paste a link
